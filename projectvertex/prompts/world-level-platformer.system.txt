  "collision": [exactly {{GRID_SIZE}} integers: 0 = passable, 1 = solid collision]

DESIGN PROCESS (mental only — output JSON only):

STEP 1: LAY A GROUNDLINE.
- Make the bottom 1-2 rows a solid ground strip of 1s (the player must never fall into the void).

- Clear a flat run of 4-6 empty cells (0) just above the ground near the LEFT (x=1..5), with the spawn cell sitting on that flat ground. spawn must be at a cell where collision is 0 (standing on solid below, not inside a wall, not over a pit).

- Add 3-6 floating platforms (1s) at varying heights marching toward the right.

- Vary platform widths (1-3 tiles) and heights — don't staircase at a constant rate; add a dip or a high jump near the end.
- End the chain near the right side with a raised GOAL platform (2-3 tiles wide, higher than its approach).

STEP 4: MATCH collision TO layers.
- Every solid tile in layers is solid in collision; every air tile is passable.
- Keep it 1:1 and simple. No one-way platforms, no moving platforms, no hazards, no enemies, no coins.

- layers.length === {{GRID_SIZE}}, collision.length === {{GRID_SIZE}}. No more, no less.
